--space-9 (96px) is the minimum gap between sections on desktop. Crowding is what kills this look, and the building itself is not crowded.

Radius — two values, nothing between

--radius-control 2px

--radius-surface 20px

Controls are near-square; images and drawers are generously rounded. Nothing sits in between, so the two never read as a mistake. Never rounded-full on a button.

Separation — hairlines, not shadows

--hairline · gold, 32%

Section rules. The one place gold is unambiguously right, because it never carries a letterform.

--hairline-soft · ink, 11%

Within-component separation, table rows, input borders.

No box-shadow anywhere in the customer-facing UI. Shadows are what make a site look like a template.
