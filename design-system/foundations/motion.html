Motion

One duration, one easing, a short permitted list and a shorter forbidden one.

The single transition

TokenValueNote
--duration480msSlower than a typical UI. The brand is unhurried.
--easingcubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)Fast out, long settle.

Permitted

  • Opacity fades
  • Rises of 8–16px on scroll entry — it should read as a fade, not a slide
  • Colour and border transitions on hover, 200–250ms

Forbidden

  • Parallax
  • Bounce and elastic easing
  • Autoplay carousels
  • Countdown timers — urgency theatre is the opposite of this brand
  • Animating width or height (animate transform and opacity)

Reduced motion

Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, --duration collapses to 1ms and every transition resolves instantly at its final state. Wired into tokens.css — components need no special handling.

Because one variable drives everything, changing the pace of the entire site is a one-line edit. That is the point of putting it in a token rather than in components.
