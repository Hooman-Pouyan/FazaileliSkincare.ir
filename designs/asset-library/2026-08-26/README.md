## Composition and visual discipline

- Photographed texture, quiet lighting, generous negative space and asymmetric pairings. No product packaging redraws, generic pink brand treatment, fake 3D gloss or stock-model collage.
- Preserve label direction. **Do not mirror branded products for RTL.** Put Persian copy in a separate panel when the photograph has no truly quiet region.
- Green belongs to photographed plants or restrained botanical artwork, not a new sage UI palette. Pink stays inside delicate blossoms, not component fills.
- The detailed botanical branch has 4,545 source paths. Prefer its PNG/WebP for display; keep SVG as an editing master. Do not animate thousands of its paths.
- The excluded flat sakura branch (S05), bright bamboo stalks (S07), cartoon flower and flat leaf are not in the usable public set. Its isolated petal studies were also rejected in final visual QA.
- P09 pink splash is a campaign alternate outside public; P11/P12 are compact-reference images, not high-resolution heroes.
- True visual exclusivity requires an original commissioned shoot or original art. These internet assets are curated, not exclusive.

Use **one botanical family per scene**. Do not combine sakura, bamboo, a tea set, a lantern and a house into a themed collage. The cultural references should support the institute's own story.

The garden and tea imagery are contextual Japanese references, not literal Kaizen symbols. A continuous-improvement narrative should be expressed through the real consultation/routine/review process. Do not add “Made in Japan,” authenticity stamps, religious seals or ingredient claims unless supported by the actual product and business evidence.

For future animation: preserve native scrolling, provide a still reduced-motion state, avoid motion behind reading text, and keep foreground/background layers few. Full garden photos are **not** depth maps or layered 3D scenes. No parallax, glow, particle system, animation or 3D model was created here.

## What was actually prepared

- Sourced the original transparent Forlle’d arrangement, avoiding regenerated text or changed packaging. It is **900×782**, not an upscaled version of the user's 1074×816 screenshot. Suitable around 450 CSS px wide at 2× density.
- Preserved downloaded masters, then made WebP renditions with source aspect ratio, a maximum 1920 px side and no raster enlargement. Original product colors and labels remain unchanged.
- Rasterized actual vector originals to transparent PNG. Removed only the background rectangle from the fine bamboo SVG. Flat petal extractions were evaluated and discarded, not delivered as premium assets.
- Saved a real alpha photographic lantern at its native **600×800**. It is suitable as a small foreground/margin object, not as a huge hero.
- Checked decoded file formats and alpha values. Several files advertised as transparent were opaque; a bamboo download with a baked checkerboard was rejected.
- No watermark removal, AI upscaling, generated product photos, fabricated textures or reconstructed product labels.
- No universal hero crops were forced. Portraits remain portraits; precise desktop/mobile art-directed crops belong to the later placement decision.
