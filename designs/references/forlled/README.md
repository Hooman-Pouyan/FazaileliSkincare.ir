# Forlle'd co-branded reference

## `forlled-workshop-invitation-1402-11-15.png`

**Provenance.** Supplied by the business owner on 2026-08-25 as a real
co-branded invitation to a joint workshop between مؤسسه مهدیه فضائلی and
Forlle'd, dated ۱۴۰۲/۱۱/۱۵. It is Forlle'd's own artwork, not ours.

**Why it is kept here.** It is the first evidence in this repository of the
visual language the brand relationship actually uses. `21-landing-composition-decisions.md`
L-5 and L-9 derive from it. It is a **reference**, not an asset: nothing in
`src/` may embed, crop or trace it, and the Forlle'd wordmark is not ours to
publish. Image rights for every brand mark remain `unknown` in
`content/brands/instagram-first-pass-2026-08-24.json`.

## What is in it

| Element | Observation |
|---|---|
| Blossom branch | Drawn as **four or five parallel hairlines forming a contour**, not a filled silhouette. Hand-drawn, irregular, no gradient |
| Blossoms | Concentric — pale pink outer disc, deep crimson centre. Warm tan/ochre secondary dots scattered between them |
| Slash marks | A repeated thin diagonal `/`, used as rhythm and as a section separator |
| 日本製 stamp | A red hanko-style square, bottom corner, reading 日本製 / MADE IN JAPAN |
| Wordmark | Forlle'd in grey, with "Expert in anti-ageing from Japan" beneath |
| Composition | Roughly 85% empty white. One motif, one stamp, one column of text. No frame, no gradient, no shadow |

## The finding that matters

The composition **corroborates `10-design-playbook.md` rather than challenging
it**. Forlle'd's own artwork is restrained to a degree the institute's Instagram
templates are not: hairlines, negative space, a single ornament, no gold frame,
no gradient. `09-brand-brief.md` observes that "the physical space is
considerably more elegant than the current graphic design" and that the website
should take its cue from the architecture. The partner brand's own material
points the same way.

The pink is the one thing not to import. `09-brand-brief.md` rejects a generic
pink-and-lavender beauty palette, and the measured institute palette stands.
See L-5 for how the form is separated from the colour, and L-9 for the boundary
between motifs this business has earned and generic japonisme.
