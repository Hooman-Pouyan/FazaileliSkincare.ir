# Content intake brief — what to prepare, and who owns each answer

Draft, 2026-08-26. This is the **question set**, not yet the column map. The
column map needs the live schema (`catalog.ts`, `content.ts`, the course
tables) and will be attached to this document once the repo is readable again.

Answers may be written in Persian. Anything not answered stays empty rather
than being invented — that rule does not bend.

## How to read the owner column

| Code | Owner | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | Mahdieh | Clinical judgement, credentials, her voice. Nobody else can supply these. |
| **H** | Hooman | Commercial decisions — price, stock, what is carried, what is promoted. |
| **C** | Storyderm catalogue | Extractable from the printed/PDF catalogue. No human time needed beyond sign-off. |
| **I** | Importer | Regulatory and licensing. IRC codes, asset permissions. |
| **D** | Derived | Computed or assembled from the above. Nobody writes it. |

**Blocking** means a surface cannot ship honestly without it. Non-blocking
fields can land empty and be filled later.

## 1. Personal branding — the about/practice surface

Volume: **one set of answers.** This is the highest-value hour anyone spends,
because nothing here exists today and none of it can come from a catalogue.

| # | Question | Owner | Blocking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Who is Mahdieh, in her own words, in 3–4 sentences? Not a CV — why she does this. | M | Yes |
| 1.2 | What is her training and qualification? Exact titles, institutions, years. | M | Yes |
| 1.3 | How many years practising, and roughly how many clients seen? | M | No |
| 1.4 | What does she believe about skin that most people in this field get wrong? | M | Yes |
| 1.5 | What is her method, named and described in 3–5 steps? | M | Yes |
| 1.6 | Who is she *not* the right practitioner for? | M | No |
| 1.7 | Where is the clinic, and what does a first visit actually involve? | M/H | Yes |
| 1.8 | Which of the 43 consented testimonials speak about *her* rather than a product? | M | No |
| 1.9 | Any professional memberships, certifications or press to display? | M | No |

Note on 1.2 — credentials are the single field most likely to attract legal
attention in Iran. Exact wording from her, verbatim, no paraphrase.

## 2. Shop hub

Volume: **one set**, plus one short block per brand and per line.

| # | Question | Owner | Blocking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | What is this shop for, in two sentences? Why buy here and not Digikala? | M/H | Yes |
| 2.2 | What are the concerns customers actually come in with? List them, most common first. | M | Yes |
| 2.3 | For each concern: a 2–3 sentence description in her voice. | M | Yes |

2.2 is worth more than it looks. The concern list becomes the taxonomy, the
navigation, and the highest-value SEO surface on the site. Get it from how she
actually talks to clients, not from a competitor's menu.

## 4. Product — hero tier

Volume: **8 products.** Full depth. This is where the practice differentiates.

| # | Question | Owner | Blocking |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Full ingredient list, INCI, in catalogue order. | C | Yes |
| 4.2 | Official usage instructions — amount, frequency, order in routine. | C | Yes |
| 4.3 | Size/volume and all variants. | C | Yes |
| 4.4 | Manufacturer's stated skin type / target concern. | C | Yes |
| 4.5 | IRC registration code. | I | Yes |
| 4.6 | Price, and whether variants price differently. | H | Yes |
| 4.7 | **Her clinical note: who is this genuinely for, and who should avoid it?** | M | Yes |
| 4.8 | What does she pair it with, and in what order? | M | No |
| 4.9 | What do people get wrong about using it? | M | No |
| 4.10 | Realistic timeframe before a customer sees a difference. | M | No |
| 4.11 | Which consented testimonials mention this product? | D | No |
| 4.12 | Packshot plus any catalogue diagrams (how-to-use steps, ingredient graphics). | C | Yes |

**4.7 is the field to protect.** It is the only thing on the page that no other
retailer of this brand can copy, and it must never be generated. It is also
distinct from 4.4 — the manufacturer's claim and her recommendation are
different statements and should never be merged into one column.

**4.1 and 4.5 are never generated, under any circumstance.** Extract verbatim
or leave empty. A hallucinated ingredient list on a cosmetics storefront is a
safety and regulatory problem, not a content-quality one.

Volume: one per course. Almost none of this comes from a catalogue — it is

someone buys, and a vague answer costs conversions.

Volume: 12–20 entries covering the questions actually asked.

|---|---|---|---|
| 8.1 | What are the top 15 questions customers ask before buying? | M | Yes |
| 8.2 | Her answer to each, in her words. | M | Yes |
| 8.3 | Which are commercial (shipping, authenticity, returns) vs clinical? | D | No |

Source these from what she is already asked daily rather than inventing a
plausible list. Commercial FAQs are yours; clinical ones are hers.

## What is *not* in this brief

Booking content is deliberately excluded — the booking design document has to
be written before its content questions make sense.

Nothing here adds a page section or a content kind. This brief describes data
for surfaces that already exist or are already planned. Any new surface — a
`product` content-spine surface, brand and line surfaces — is a schema decision
that goes in a decision document first.

---

## Suggested order of attack

1. **Section 2.2** — the concern list. Everything else hangs off it, it takes
   twenty minutes, and it is the taxonomy the whole shop is organised by.
2. **Section 1** — the practice surface. One sitting, entirely unblocked, and
   the site currently has nothing here at all.
3. **Section 5** — the 42 thin entries, straight from the catalogue. Mostly
   mechanical, and it makes the shop look complete.
4. **Section 4, fields 4.1–4.6 and 4.12** — hero product facts from the
   catalogue and from you.
5. **Section 4.7** — her clinical notes on 8 products. Ninety seconds of talking
   per product if writing is the obstacle.
6. **Sections 6 and 7** — courses and packages.
7. **Section 8** — FAQ.

Steps 1, 3 and 4 are unblocked today. Step 5 is the one that decides whether
this reads as a practice or as a reseller.
